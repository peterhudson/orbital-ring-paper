# Program Brief

## Mission
Free bulk access to orbit from dependence on chemical rockets by developing electrically powered active-support infrastructure.

## Why this matters
Orbit is the gateway to everything beyond Earth: energy, industry, science, defense, planetary resilience, and eventually civilization-scale growth.

If every ton must ride a rocket, space remains expensive, intermittent, and bottlenecked.
If mass can ride infrastructure, space becomes industrial.

## Candidate architectures

### 1. Launch loop
A high-speed circulating mass in a magnetically supported sheath creates dynamic support for an elevated track, allowing payloads to be accelerated to orbital or near-orbital velocity.

### 2. Orbital ring
A rapidly moving ring system in low Earth orbit supports stationary structures or lifting elements through active magnetic and dynamic interaction.

### 3. Atlas tower / active support tower
A dynamically stabilized tall structure reaches extreme altitude without requiring ordinary compressive material performance across its full height.

### 4. Neighboring concepts worth comparing
- Skyhook / rotating tether systems
- Momentum exchange tethers
- Electromagnetic launch assist + upper-stage hybrids
- Space elevator precursor infrastructure
- High-altitude anchored or buoyant support systems

## Evaluation criteria
Every candidate should be scored against:
- Physics feasibility
- Materials feasibility
- Control-system feasibility
- Failure modes and safety envelope
- Energy efficiency
- Capital cost
- Operating cost
- Construction sequence
- Maintainability
- Geographic and political constraints
- Throughput potential
- Upgrade path to large-scale orbital industry

## Program posture
We are not here to romantically defend a favorite concept.
We are here to identify the fastest credible path to mass access to orbit.
