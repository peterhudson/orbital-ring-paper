# Phase 1 Roadmap: From Vision to Credible Architecture

## Phase 1 objective
Produce a technically literate down-selection of candidate non-rocket orbital access systems and identify the best path for deeper design work.

## Workstream A: Requirements
Define the target system requirements:
- Payload classes: kilograms, tons, tens of tons, hundreds of tons
- Target destinations: suborbital, LEO insertion, transfer staging, orbital ring feed
- Cadence: launches per day/week
- Safety envelope over land/ocean
- Electrical power demand and sourcing assumptions
- Acceptable g-loads for cargo and humans

## Workstream B: Concept catalog
Create structured design sheets for:
- Launch loop
- Orbital ring
- Atlas tower
- Skyhook / tether variants
- Hybrid architectures

For each, capture:
- Mechanism of support
- Mechanism of acceleration
- Required materials
- Required control systems
- Geographic footprint
- Key single-point failures
- Construction prerequisites

## Workstream C: Constraint-first analysis
For each concept, identify first-order limits:
- Stress / specific strength limits
- Thermal limits
- Vibration and dynamic stability issues
- Power electronics and magnetic field limits
- Vacuum, atmospheric, and aerodynamic issues
- Debris and weather exposure
- Maintenance access challenges

## Workstream D: Deployment pathway
Map how a civilization actually builds one:
- Prototype scale
- Pilot scale
- Revenue-generating intermediate uses
- Path to full-scale operational system

## Workstream E: Decision framework
Build a comparison matrix and force explicit judgments instead of hand-wavy optimism.

## Immediate next deliverables
1. A comparison table of the top concepts
2. A blocker list for each concept
3. A recommendation on what to study first
4. A research bibliography with primary sources where possible

## My recommendation right now
Start with a structured comparison of launch loop, orbital ring, and Atlas tower, with skyhook systems as a reality check.

Why:
- Launch loop may be the nearest thing to a buildable active-support mass-to-orbit concept
- Orbital ring may be the most civilization-shaping long-term architecture
- Atlas tower is conceptually seductive but likely brutal on dynamics and control
- Skyhooks help benchmark what can be done sooner with less megascale infrastructure
